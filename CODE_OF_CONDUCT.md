# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, accept
constructive feedback, and protect users' privacy. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior is harmful to the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer through the repository's security contact. Reports will be handled
confidentially and reviewed fairly.
